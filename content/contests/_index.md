People build robots for all sorts of reasons. For many, there is the simple satisfaction of making something that can perform a task, for others the fun is in the design and build rather than the end product.

Many builders want to pit their robot against others in some kind of challenging task.

There are many contest challenges for the small robot builder. UKMARS supports five different challenges. Three of these are based in a miniature maze and two are based on following a line marked out on the ground.

## **Maze Based Challenges** {#Maze_Based_Challenges}

All these challenges take place in the confines of a 16 x 16 cell maze, either a full size one with 18cm cells or a half size one with 9cm cells

![Title](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/micromouse-uk-nov-2014-160x160.png)

#### Micromouse {#Micromouse}

The longest-running robot contest in the world. Autonomous robots solve a complex maze and then run though it as fast as possible.

[Find Out More](/contests/micromouse/)![Title](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/TechFest2018_MazeCloseUp-160x160.jpg)

#### Wall Following {#Wall_Following}

These robots use the same maze but are less intelligent, finding their way to the centre by following one wall of the maze all the way to the centre.

[Find Out More](/contests/wall-follower/)![Title](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/time-trial-robot-uk-2010-160x160.png)

#### Time Trials {#Time_Trials}

A test of speed, power and control. Robots must run around the outside of the maze at the fastest possible speed by any means necessary. Simple but great fun.

[Find Out More](/contests/time-trials/)

## **Line Following Challenges** {#Line_Following_Challenges}

Robots must follow a contrasting line drawn out on the floor.

![Title](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/line-follower-robot-japan-2016-320x320.png)

##### Line Following {#Line_Following}

The basic task is to follow the line as fast as possible. Smarter robots learn the course layout to optimise performance through the different sections.

[Find Out More](/contests/line-follower/)![Title](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/drag-race-2017-finish-line-320x213.png)

##### Drag Race {#Drag_Race}

A head-to-head knockout contest where robots follow a fixed-length straight track at top speed while coming to a controlled stop at the end.

[Find Out More](/contests/drag-race/)![Pursuit](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/pursuit-course-example-320x211.png)

##### Pursuit {#Pursuit}

Pairs of robots chase each other around a fixed course while following the line. The aim is to enter the same zone as the robot ahead of you.

[Find Out More](/contests/contest-rules/pursuit-competition/)
