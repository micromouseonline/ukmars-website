Registration is now **Closed** for our Autumn Competition for 2025 being held in Hazlemere, High Wycombe at Hazlemere **Youth Centre**, Rose Avenue, Hazlemere, High Wycombe, Buckinghamshire, HP15 7UB

We may be able to accept some late registrations on the day if previously registered entries complete within the time allowed for our contests.

Note: this is the youth centre not the community centre that we have used in the past which is very close by.

Doors will open for setup at 08:30 and we aim to start the first contest at 10:00

Here is the indicative [Programme](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025AutumnDraftProgramme.pdf)

**The closing time for registrations is 9pm on Sunday 28th September. We cannot guarantee registration on the day if contests are fully booked.  
**

We will be hosting the following contests for junior and senior entries:

  - Line following(half size)
  - Wall Following (classic and half size)
  - Maze Solving(classic and half size)
  - Drag Race
  - Pursuit Contest

When attending competitions please ensure that you read and comply with our [**Safeguarding Policy**](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/SafeguardingPolicyAndProceduresv1p3.pdf) and follow this **[Guidance](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/GuidanceToCompetitorsandSpectators.pdf)**
