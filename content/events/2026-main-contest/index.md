The UKMARS main competition is being held in Saturday 18<sup>th</sup> April from 10:00am at:

King Edward VI school in Stratford upon Avon.  
Access from Chapel Lane, post code CV37 6BE.

**Registration is now open.**

First, register who will be attending:

[**Attendee Registration Form**](https://forms.gle/8vcDyp9MyFweeR1N8)

Second, register the robots that you will be running:

[**Robot Registration Form**](https://forms.gle/RE6DzMyCWZ65yvvT8)

Registration will close at 9pm on Saturday April 11th.

Please note that we are unable to admit contestants or spectators that are under the age of 18 unless they are accompanied by a responsible adult.

We will be running the following contests for junior and senior entries:

  - Line following(half size)
  - Pursuit
  - Wall Following (classic and half size)
  - Maze Solving(classic and half size)
  - Drag Race

We will also have a track available for contestants to try out the new Pursuit event.

The nearest airport is Birmingham International BHX.

The nearest train station is Stratford upon Avon station – Chiltern Railways.

There is car parking at the school car park which will have the barrier open. It is accessed from Chapel Lane in Stratford upon Avon post code CV37 6BE. Chapel lane is one way and so needs to be entered from the High Street end. The car park is pay and display and the entrance to the car park on the right as you go down Chapel Lane

The competition is being held in the Gymnasium and the entrance to the Gym is immediately off the car park area.
