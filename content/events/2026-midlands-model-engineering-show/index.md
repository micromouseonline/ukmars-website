UKMARS will be exhibiting again in 2026 at the Midlands Model Engineering Show. Everything you need to know about the show is [here](https://www.meridienneexhibitions.co.uk/events/midlands-model-engineering-exhibition/visit)

We will be demonstrating micro-mouse robots doing line following, wall folllowing and maze solving on reduced size test tracks. Micro-mice expert builders will be on hand to answer all of your question on how to get involved or how to build your first micro-mouse.
