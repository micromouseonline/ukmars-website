We had a great couple of days at the Megacon exhibition in Manchester on Saturday 26th and Sunday 27th July including a Dalek and Tardis pursuit. We ran contests on the Saturday and demonstrations on the Sunday.

Congratulations to everyone that entered robots, we saw some exciting contests and some new robots running for the first time.

Here are the results for the robots that completed contests (results are only published for entries that succussfully complete a challenge and obtain a run time).
