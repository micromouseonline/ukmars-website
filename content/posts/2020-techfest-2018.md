TechFest 2018 and UK Micromouse was hosted by UKMARS and Birmingham City University (BCU) at Millennium Point, Birmingham, UK. on Saturday 9 June 2018.

Competitions hosted were:

  - Line follower
  - Drag Race
  - Micromouse
      - Non-Contact Wall Follower (Junior and Senior)
      - Maze Solver (Junior, Intermediate and Senior)
      - Time Trail
