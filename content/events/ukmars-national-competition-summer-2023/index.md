The National Competition this year is in Worcester at Drakes Broughton Village Hall, Walcot Lane, Pershore, Drakes Broughton, Worcestershire, WR10 2AJ.

This is 6 miles from M5 J7 and 3 miles from Worcestershire Parkway railway station. Nearest airport is Birmingham BHX.

### Here is the indicative [Programme](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/ProgrammeSummer23.pdf) and the [Track Schedule](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/TrackScheduleSummer23.pdf)

#### On-line Registration is now closed**  
**

The deadline for on-line registrations was Monday 25 June.

You can register to attend on the day. We may not be able to accept robot entries on the day if competitions are fully booked.

We will be hosting the following contests for junior and senior entries:

  - Line following(classic and half size)
  - Wall Following (classic and half size)
  - Maze Solving(classic and half size)
  - Drag Race

When attending competitions please ensure that you read and comply with our [**Safeguarding Policy**](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/SafeguardingPolicyAndProceduresv1p1.pdf) and follow this **[Guidance](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/GuidanceToCompetitorsandSpectators.pdf)**
