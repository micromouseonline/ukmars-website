## MECHANICAL PARTS {#MECHANICAL_PARTS}

You will need the following parts to complete the robot:

Two motors, wheels and tyres, mounts to hold the motors in place, optionally encoders, a battery and battery connector, nuts and bolts to hold the motors on place and support the Mezzanine or sensor boards, and two skids to keep the robot level. Below are details of each of these things:

<table>
<colgroup>
<col style="width: 33%" />
<col style="width: 33%" />
<col style="width: 33%" />
</colgroup>
<tbody>
<tr class="odd">
<td><p>Motors</p></td>
<td><p>You need 2 Micro Metal Gearmotors with a gear ratio ideally of around 20:1 (600 rpm) with extended back shafts if you plan to add encoders, or with encoders already included. Polulo do 15:1 or 30:1 (800rpm or 400 rpm) 6V motors with encoders fitted which would be OK</p></td>
<td><p>Polulo or Ebay or AliExpress are good sources</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><p>Encoders and encoder cables</p></td>
<td><p>You can add an encoder disk to an extended back shaft micro gear motor. These are sometimes available from Pi Hut but more often now the motors are sold with an encoder fitted You will need two 6 pin 1mm pitch JST-SH cables to connect the motor encoder to the main board. An adaptor is used to convert the JST-SH cable to 2.54 pitch to connect it to J1 and J2</p></td>
<td><p>Cable – Pi Hut “JST-SH cable – 6 pin” </p>
<p>Adaptor – Pi Hut “Breakout for 6-pin JST SH-Style Connector – Side Entry”</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td><p>Wheels and Tyres</p></td>
<td><p>Pololu Wheel 32×7mm Pair available in black or white or you could 3D print some and use O rings for tyres</p></td>
<td><p>Pi Hut SKU: POL-1087 or SKU: POL-1088</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><p>Motor Mounts</p></td>
<td><p>You need 2 motor mounts. They can be 3D printed if you have the capability or you can buy the Polulo ones</p></td>
<td><p>Pi Hut code SKU: POL-989</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td><p>Battery and connector</p></td>
<td><p>The battery should be a PP3 9Volt block Lithium Ion rechargeable one with micro USB charging socket and ideally charge level LEDs on the side.</p>
<p>The battery Connector lead is from Rapid</p></td>
<td><p>Search on Ebay or elsewhere for “PP3 Block Li-ion USB rechargeable battery” Ideally 1200mAH capacity or similar Standard PP3 9V batteries will not deliver enough current.</p>
<p>Rapid Online Order Code: 18-0092</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><p>Nuts and bolts</p></td>
<td><p>If you are using 3D printed mounts which have 3mm holes in them you need 4 M3 x 30mm flat head polyamide bolts. Pack of M3 polyamide nuts 8 required</p>
<p>M3 x 10mm plastic spacers or 10mm piece of plastic tubing</p></td>
<td><p>Rapid Online Order Code: 51-3852</p>
<p>Rapid-Online Order Code: 51-3437</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td><p>Skids</p></td>
<td><p>A 12mm long M3 bolt and 2 nuts will work for the front skid</p></td>
<td><p>Rapid Online Order Code: 33-7036</p></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
