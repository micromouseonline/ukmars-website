Each month, members of UKMARS gather online for a virtual meeting. Most months there will be a talk on some robot-related subject. After that, there is generally an opportunity for discussion around the topic of the talk and time for updates and news from members.

This month we hope to hear from Peter Harrison who will be describing how he has made the UKMARSBOT robot run in a classic micromouse maze.
