Our Autumn Competition this year is in Hazlemere HIgh Wycombe at Hazlemere **Youth Centre**, Rose Avenue, Hazlemere, High Wycombe, Buckinghamshire, HP15 7UB

Note: this is the youth centre not the community centre that we have used in the past which is very close by.

**Here is the indicative [Programme](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Autumn23programmeV2.pdf) for the day.**

**Here is an indication of when [Tracks](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Autumn23trackSchedule.pdf) will be available for practice.**

**Doors will open for setup at 08:00 and we aim to start the first contest at 10:00**

**On-line registration is now closed.  
**

If you have forgotten to register you may be able to register on the day however we cannot guarentee that this will be possible if the contests are fully booked.

We will be hosting the following contests for junior and senior entries:

  - Line following(classic and half size)
  - Wall Following (classic and half size)
  - Maze Solving(classic and half size)
  - Drag Race

When attending competitions please ensure that you read and comply with our [**Safeguarding Policy**](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/SafeguardingPolicyAndProceduresv1p1.pdf) and follow this **[Guidance](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/GuidanceToCompetitorsandSpectators.pdf)**
