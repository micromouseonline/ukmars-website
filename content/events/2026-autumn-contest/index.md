We will be holding our Autumn Contest in 2026 in Hazlemere on Sunday 4 October 2026 following a practice day at the same venue on Saturday 3 October.

Hazlemere Youth Centre, Rose Avenue, Hazlemere, High Wycombe, Buckinghamshire, HP15 7UB
