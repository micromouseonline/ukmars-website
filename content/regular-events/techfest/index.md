This is our annual robotics festival. It includes the main UK Micromouse Contest as well as a number of other challenges for the small robot builder.

The 2023 it is in Worcester – [see here for details](/events/ukmars-national-competition-summer-2023/), but previously it was been kindly held in conjunction with Birmingham City University. This is a big day for everyone.

**The [2023 event](/events/ukmars-national-competition-summer-2023/) is scheduled for 1 July 2022.**
