On the first Thursday of each month at 20:00 UK time members have the chance to catch up with a video meeting. There is always an opportunity for members to ask for help with any aspect of robot building and take advantage of the wealth of knowledge available.

There is also the opportunity for “show and tell” so members can share their current developments with others on the call. These are informal meetings with a typical outline agenda:

  - Welcome to the new members
  - Q\&A session for robot-related issues:
  - – Do you need help with resolving, fixing, or choosing something?
  - Show and Tell:
  - – Do you have any new projects you would like to share with others?
  - Call for volunteers to host future meetings
  - Any Other Business

We take it in turns to chair these video meetings, joining instructions are emailed to all members by the chair of each meeting. The people chairing future meetings are:
