We had a great day of contests on Saturday 4 October in Hazlemere. It was good to see new members and their robots running for the first time. Congratulations to everyone that entered robots, we saw some exciting contests with some twists in the drag racing and 3 junior entries to the maze solving contest.

Here are the results for the contests.
