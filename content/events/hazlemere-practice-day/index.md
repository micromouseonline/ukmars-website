This year we are holding a practice day before our Autumn competition day in Hazlemere. Practice day is on Saturday 3 October with the competition day on Sunday 4 October.

Hazlemere Youth Centre, Rose Avenue, Hazlemere, High Wycombe, Buckinghamshire, HP15 7UB

**Doors will open at 9:00 on both days with contests commencing on Sunday from 09:30**

The first contests to run will be Pursuit and Line Following followed by all the remaining contests with Classic Maze Solving commencing at 13:00

**REGISTRATION NOW CLOSED**

Registration is now closed for the practice day on 3 October and the contest day  on 4 October 2026,, we may be able to accept some late registrations on the day if registered entries complete within the time allowed for our contests.

Please note that we are unable to admit contestants or spectators that are under the age of 18 unless they are accompanied by a responsible adult.

We will be running the following contests for junior and senior entries:

Line following(half size)  
Wall Following (classic and half size)  
Maze Solving(classic and half size)  
Drag Race

Pursuit Challenge
