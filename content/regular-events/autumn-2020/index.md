Each autumn, UKMARS holds a friendly meeting and competition event. We stage all of the normal contest challenges but in a smaller and less formal setting that the main Techfest event in the summer.

The autumn event lets contestants catch up on competitor’s progress and test out the improvements they have made over the summer. This opportunity gets everyone set up for the winter so that they can carry on their robotics development, freshly inspired, during those long, dark winter evening.

Come along and have a chat or run your robot.
