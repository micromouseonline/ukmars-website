Here are the results for the Maze contests run on Sunday 4 October. It turned out to be a tough maze for many competitors with only 11 senior classic Micromouse succeeding from 23 entries. Great to see the single junior entry getting to the centre of the maze.

The half size maze solving and half size wall follower successful entries were very impressive. The maze presented similar challenges for constructors of half size Micromice with only 1 entry succeeding from 5 entries for half size maze solving.
