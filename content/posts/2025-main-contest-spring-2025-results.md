We had a great day of contests on Saturday 5 April in Stratford-Upon-Avon. Congratulations to everyone that entered robots, we saw some exciting contests with some twists in the drag racing and for the first time had 2 robots achieve identical winning times in a contest.

We previewed the pursuit challenge which looks very promising as an engaging event and will be refining this at Minos in the summer.

Here are the results for the robots that completed contests (results are only published for entries that succussfully complete a challenge and obtain a run time).
