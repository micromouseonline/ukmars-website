Registration is now closed for our Autumn Competition for 2024 once again being held in Hazlemere, High Wycombe at Hazlemere **Youth Centre**, Rose Avenue, Hazlemere, High Wycombe, Buckinghamshire, HP15 7UB

Note: this is the youth centre not the community centre that we have used in the past which is very close by.

**Doors will open for setup at 08:00 and we aim to start the first contest at 10:00**

This year we have a pop-up cafe at the venue that will open shortly after doors open and will be available throughout the day. Hot and cold drinks and food will be available.

**Here is the indicative [Programme](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2024AutumnProgramme.pdf)**

**The closing time for registrations was 9pm on Monday 30th September.You may still be able to register on the day. We cannot guarantee registration on the day if contests are fully booked.  
**

We will be hosting the following contests for junior and senior entries:

  - Line following(half size)
  - Wall Following (classic and half size)
  - Maze Solving(classic and half size)
  - Drag Race

When attending competitions please ensure that you read and comply with our [**Safeguarding Policy**](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/SafeguardingPolicyAndProceduresv1p1.pdf) and follow this **[Guidance](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/GuidanceToCompetitorsandSpectators.pdf)**
