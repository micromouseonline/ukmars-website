***Updated 17:05 on 23/06/2022*** This year’s summer contest is being held at STEAMhouse, Belmont Works, 4 Belmont Row, Birmingham B4 7RQ starting at 10:00. **Doors open at 09:30**

It will be great to see the robots running again, here is an indicative timetable of events **[Programme](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/ProgrammeOfEvents.pdf)**  and the availability of the tracks can be seen in this [**Track Schedule**](/ukmars/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/TrackScheduleV2.pdf) Please note these timings are approximate and may vary on the day.

STEAMhouse is a brand new venue immediately adjacent to Millennium Point. You can find out more here [**https://steamhouse.org.uk/contact/**](https://steamhouse.org.uk/contact/) scroll down the page for a map and directions for different modes of transport.

For people that have attended previous TechFest contests the best car park is still the Millennium Point car park which is just over the road from STEAMhouse.

Register your robots for the contests here [**registration\_form**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScCXm6fkHIOgUYqOctWUMSu8lGSEOendZ2nW0sdDnvtcg9SJQ/viewform?usp=sf_link) **no later than 19:00 on Wednesday 22 June 2022.**

We need to let Birmingham Ciry University know who is expected to attend, so if you are planning to attend and are not registering any robots, email chairman@ukmars.org and advise the names of your expected attendees. Again **no later than 19:00 on Wednesday 22 June 2022.**
